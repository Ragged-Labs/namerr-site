SUPPORT THE PROJECT

Good software deserves
a great community.

Namerr is being developed as a free-to-use, self-hosted tool. Community feedback and voluntary contributions can help keep it useful, independent and actively maintained.

Voluntary support is being prepared.

Namerr is intended to remain free to use. Donations can help fund future development, but they do not buy feature priority, guaranteed support or early access. Neither donation option is active during private prelaunch.
