What happens before we open downloads?

01. Public release package

A verified, publicly accessible package and clear version information — not a broken link to the private development repository.

02. Installation and upgrade guides

Tested instructions for new installs and existing Unraid deployments without unintended changes to appdata or media mappings.

03. Security, licensing and support checks

Clear distribution rights, safe configuration defaults, known limitations and a way for community users to report issues.

Keep using the update procedure and image references that are proven for your current installation. A new website does not require changing your container or its mapped folders.
