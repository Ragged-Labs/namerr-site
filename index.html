WHY NAMERR?

A growing collection.
Without the growing mess.

Movies, shows, artwork and other files can become a puzzle. Namerr is designed to bring clarity to that puzzle, combining useful metadata with transparent review instead of silently making decisions about your library.

THE PLAN

Purpose-built for
the way you collect.

Here's the direction we're building toward. Features may change during development and are not yet available as a public release.

▧INITIAL RELEASE FOCUS

Movies & TV

Inspect titles, episodes, seasons and related files, then organise them into a consistent library structure.

✓INITIAL RELEASE FOCUS

Guided review

See proposed names and moves before applying them. Keep ambiguous matches and risky operations under your control.

◇PLANNED EXPANSION

Connected metadata

Use compatible media services and available metadata sources to help identify what you have.

✳FUTURE ROADMAP

Intelligent assistance

Optional local or connected AI to explain matches, find inconsistencies and suggest safer actions.

♫FUTURE ROADMAP

More than video

Plans include music, audiobooks, books and photos as the product matures.

⌂SELF-HOSTING FIRST

Your library, your rules

Designed around local control, reviewable actions and clear installation and upgrade guidance.
