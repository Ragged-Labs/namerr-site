WEBSITE PRIVACY

Privacy without surprises.

This information relates to Namerr's public Coming Soon website, not to the application, which has not been publicly released.

What this website collects

Our website is static HTML and CSS. We do not currently use user accounts, forms, browser analytics, tracking cookies, advertising pixels or embedded payment services. We do not use an email signup form.

Hosting and third-party links

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor connection details and technical logs under its own privacy terms. Links to our GitHub organisation or X profile lead to their own services with independent privacy policies.

Downloads and donations

No public installers or Bitcoin receiving addresses are available yet. The Support page offers an outbound link and a locally hosted QR code for the Namerr Buy Me a Coffee profile. Scanning the QR code or clicking the link opens that external service; any payment is processed by Buy Me a Coffee under its own terms and privacy policy, not by our website. Donations are voluntary and confer no entitlement to features or support.

Contact

For privacy or website enquiries, contact contact@raggedlabs.media at RaggedLabs Media.
